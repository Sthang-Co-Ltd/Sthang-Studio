## Included accepted changes

- Protect unfinished caption and Accuracy edits, retain recoverable browser drafts, and reject stale saves without overwriting newer saved work.
- Find saved projects with search, sorting and pages; surface unreadable or missing project storage with a safe recheck.
- Replace compatible footage while preserving reviewed captions and timing; require explicit acceptance for edited audio and retain recoverable evidence.
- Preview bulk corrections, repair export issues by caption, retain export choices during repairs, and distinguish historical outputs from current work.
- Improve native caption preview recovery and bounds handling, word timing, waveform navigation, dialog focus and deliberate playback seeks.

The release preparation introduced no additional provider, telemetry category, third-party dependency or protected-artwork change.
