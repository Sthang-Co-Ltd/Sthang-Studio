Sthang Studio 0.90.0 - Public Beta

Turn on Word highlighting in Appearance to sync unresolved captions locally and save usable timings. Review remaining captions in
Fine Timing or undo the timing changes. For short cues, Sync words can suggest a caption-edge repair to audition and apply.
Timing repairs that change caption edges require a fresh review of the affected captions.

Open optional Accuracy context before generation to add names and wording guidance for the current project. Preview Find and
Replace changes before applying them. The Graphite export workspace makes preset feedback and previous exports easier to find.
Editor feedback is clearer around saving, uploads, loading workspaces, and adjusting appearance.

Requirements: Windows 10 or 11 x64, or Apple Silicon macOS 12.3+, internet for initial setup and AI wording, and a Gemini
Developer API key. The Mac installer provides a local Finder launcher.
Studio requests store: false for Gemini interactions. Audio goes through the Gemini Files API, where Google controls retention
for up to 48 hours. Word sync processes audio and wording locally. Contributor and analytics transfers each require explicit opt-in.

Recovery assets, each with a matching SHA-256 file:
- Sthang-Studio-Windows-v0.90.0.zip
- Sthang-Studio-macOS-Apple-Silicon-v0.90.0.zip

In Studio, choose Check for updates, then Download & verify and Install & restart. The manual installer is the recovery route
and delivers stable-broker changes. Existing Apple Silicon installations need the 0.85.6 or newer bootstrap for in-app updates.
