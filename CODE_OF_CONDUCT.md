# Community code of conduct

Sthang Studio welcomes respectful contributions from people of different
backgrounds and experience levels, including creators who are not professional
software developers.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific when discussing bugs or design choices.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Make room for Khmer-language and Cambodia-specific product context rather than
  treating English-language assumptions as universal.
- Protect other people's privacy. Do not publish private media, project data,
  credentials, or personal information while reproducing an issue.
- Keep contribution discussions relevant to improving Sthang Studio.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disruption, doxxing,
sexualized harassment, or publishing another person's private information are
not acceptable in Sthang Studio community spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict
participation when behavior undermines a safe, productive project community.

For a private conduct report, contact Sthang through the private contact method
published on https://sthang.app/. Do not use a public issue when the report
contains personal or sensitive information.

This code applies to Sthang Studio's repository discussions, issues, pull
requests, and other project-managed community spaces.
